📦 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)
📌 Project Overview

This project is based on the analysis of Amazon sales data using Power BI to extract meaningful business insights. The dashboard provides a complete and interactive view of key performance indicators such as revenue, orders, shipment status, category-wise performance, state-wise distribution, and product size trends.

The main objective of this project is to understand product demand, sales performance, and logistics efficiency to support data-driven decisions in an e-commerce environment.

📊 Key Metrics (From Dashboard)

Total Revenue: 48M

Total Orders: 80K

Units Sold: 72K

Successful Shipments: 68K

Date Range: March 2022 – May 2022

📈 Dashboard Insights
✅ Sales Trend Analysis

Monthly sales trend shows variation between March, April, and May.

Helps in identifying peak and low sales months.

✅ Category-Wise Sales

T-Shirts are the top-selling category (~24.57M)

Followed by Shirts (12.82M) and Blazers (6.56M)

Lowest contribution: Shoes & Socks

✅ Top 10 Shipment States

Highest shipments recorded in:

Maharashtra

Karnataka

Uttar Pradesh

Telangana

Tamil Nadu

Delhi

Kerala

West Bengal

Andhra Pradesh

Haryana

This helps in understanding location-based demand.

✅ Sales Trend by Product Size

Highest sales seen in M, L, and XL

Very low demand for 6XL, 5XL and Free size

This insight is useful for optimizing stock inventory.

✅ Courier Status

Maximum orders are Shipped successfully

Very small number of Cancelled, Unshipped, and On the Way orders

✅ Courier Partner

Amazon Fulfillment handles most deliveries

Merchant Fulfillment is used less frequently
